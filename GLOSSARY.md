# Facelove storefront

The language used for the shop's landing pages and how they sell.

## Language

**Hero product**:
The single product a landing page exists to sell. On `product.landing-01` it is the Changing Foundation Stick.
_Avoid_: Main product, featured product

**Bundle**:
A larger quantity of the hero product offered at a lower unit price (for example 1 stick vs 3 sticks).
_Avoid_: Upsell, pack, tier

**Cross-sell**:
A different product offered next to the hero product so the shopper buys both. Shown to shoppers as "Complete your routine".
_Avoid_: Upsell, recommended products, related products, add-on

**Free gift**:
A product given at no charge once the order qualifies. Never offered as a Cross-sell.
_Avoid_: Mystery gift (that is one specific free gift product), bonus
