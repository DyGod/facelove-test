# Hypotheses

Why we made each conversion change. Each entry says what we believed, what we changed because of it, and what would prove us wrong. We don't run a formal test for every entry. These notes are here so the reasoning isn't lost.

## H1. One Black Friday sale block instead of two

Shipped 2026-11-18. Page: `product.landing-01`.

The buy box showed the Black Friday sale twice. The countdown ("Price increases in") sat above the bundle picker. A second block with a progress bar ("Selling fast", 87%) and another countdown sat below the add to cart button. They said the same thing and pushed the guarantee and gift list further apart.

We kept the top one and removed the bottom one.

- The shopper picks a bundle and price right under the top timer. A deadline next to the price makes the deal feel real at the moment they choose. The bottom block showed up after that choice, mostly to people already scrolling past.
- On mobile the bottom block sat a full screen below the button. Most shoppers never reached it before they bought or left.
- The bottom block's 87% bar was a number we typed in, not real stock. It carried the same trust and legal risk as the "Low stock" badge we removed the same day.

What we expect: conversion rate holds or rises slightly, because the guarantee now sits closer to the button.

What would prove us wrong: conversion rate on this page drops in the two weeks after the change, compared with the two weeks before.

Open item. The top timer says "Price increases in". That has to be true. If the price does not go up on 2026-12-01, change the label to "Sale ends in".

## H2. Show the free gifts once, inside the bundle card

Shipped 2026-11-18. Page: `product.landing-01`.

Free shipping, the mystery gift and the primer showed in two places. One list sat inside the 2 stick card in the bundle picker. A row of tiles sat below the add to cart button, greyed out with a lock until the shopper picked the 2 stick bundle.

We kept the list inside the card and removed the tiles.

- The card is where the shopper compares 1 stick with 2 sticks. Seeing the gifts there makes the bigger bundle look like the better deal at the moment of choice. The tiles came after the button, once the choice was already made.
- The card list is the one that adds the gifts to the cart. With auto-add on, picking the 2 stick card puts the primer and mystery gift in the cart for free. The tiles were only a picture. Removing the card list would have meant shoppers no longer got their gifts.
- The two lists said the same thing in two styles, which made the buy box longer and harder to scan on mobile.

What we expect: the share of orders on the 2 stick bundle holds or rises, and more shoppers see the guarantee because it sits right under the button.

What would prove us wrong: the share of 2 stick orders drops in the two weeks after the change. The locked tiles may have nudged 1 stick shoppers to switch. If that happens, put the locked tiles back above the button instead of below it.
